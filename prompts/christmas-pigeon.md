Christmas Pigeon — Image Transformation Prompt

Objective

Transform a user-provided photograph into a humorous Christmas-themed image.

First, determine whether the photograph contains a visible pigeon.

There are two possible outcomes:

1. Pigeon present: Dress the existing pigeon in a festive knitted Christmas sweater featuring an original bird-related holiday pun, complemented by one or two Christmas accessories.
2. No pigeon present: Preserve the original photograph and add a large, funny, lo-fi computer-style text overlay about the absence of a pigeon.

Never introduce a pigeon that was not present in the original photograph.

The central creative principle is understated visual absurdity: an ordinary pigeon, in an ordinary environment, inexplicably dressed for Christmas—or an ordinary photograph humorously acknowledging that no pigeon was found.

1. Inspect the Original Photograph

Before making any changes, examine the uploaded image and determine whether a pigeon is visibly present.

A pigeon may be partially obscured, distant, or appear anywhere within the frame.

Only treat a pigeon as present if it is reasonably identifiable in the original photograph.

Do not infer the presence of a pigeon merely because the setting is a park, sidewalk, rooftop, or another location where pigeons are commonly found.

Do not mistake unrelated objects, shadows, or other birds for pigeons.

If a pigeon is present

Follow Sections 2–6 to transform the existing pigeon.

If no pigeon is present

Skip all pigeon clothing and accessory instructions.

Follow Section 7 to add a humorous text overlay to the unchanged photograph.

These outcomes are mutually exclusive. Never generate, insert, or invent a pigeon.

2. Preserve the Original Photograph

Image preservation is the highest priority.

Treat the uploaded photograph as the source of truth. Modify only what is necessary to add Christmas clothing and accessories convincingly.

* Preserve the pigeon’s distinctive appearance, including feather colors, patterns, eyes, beak, proportions, and anatomy.
* Preserve the original environment, including architecture, vegetation, background objects, weather, and season.
* Maintain the original lighting, shadows, perspective, camera angle, and depth of field.
* Preserve the pigeon’s original pose, gaze, and expression wherever possible.
* Do not relocate the pigeon or introduce a new background.
* Do not add snow, Christmas decorations, lights, or other environmental elements that were not present in the original photograph.
* Avoid unnecessary changes to the composition.

Minor adjustments to the pigeon are permitted only when necessary to convincingly accommodate the outfit.

The goal is to make the transformed image feel like the same photograph, taken at the same location and under the same conditions.

3. Natural Pigeon Appearance and Behavior

Let the pigeon look and behave like an ordinary pigeon.

* Preserve its natural expression and gaze.
* Do not intentionally make the pigeon appear serious, grumpy, confused, happy, or excited.
* Avoid anthropomorphic facial expressions, gestures, or poses.
* Maintain realistic bird anatomy and proportions.
* Allow the pigeon’s existing appearance and posture to contribute naturally to the humor.

The pigeon does not need to communicate a particular emotion.

The outfit is the joke, not the pigeon’s expression.

4. Christmas Sweater and Slogan

Dress the pigeon in a detailed, realistic knitted Christmas sweater featuring a prominently displayed, original Christmas-themed bird pun.

Slogan creation

Invent a clever, funny slogan that combines Christmas or winter holiday themes with birds, pigeons, or bird-related behavior.

* Create an original pun rather than relying on familiar or commonly repeated phrases.
* Draw inspiration from Christmas songs, holiday traditions, seasonal expressions, bird anatomy, pigeon behavior, and urban bird life.
* Favor playful, unexpected, slightly ridiculous wordplay.
* Keep the slogan short enough to be clearly readable on the sweater, ideally 2–6 words.
* Avoid generic holiday greetings that contain no bird-related humor.
* Do not default to obvious or overused puns.
* Choose a slogan that feels fresh, surprising, and amusing.
* Vary the style of wordplay rather than repeatedly substituting the same bird-related words into familiar Christmas phrases.

Generate several possible slogans internally, then select one for its humor, originality, and readability.

Do not display the alternatives or explain the selection.

Sweater design

* Use authentic knitted fabric with visible stitches, ribbing, and natural texture.
* Incorporate traditional Christmas patterns such as snowflakes, Christmas trees, holly, candy canes, or miniature birds.
* Integrate the invented slogan into the knitted fabric rather than overlaying digital text.
* Render the selected slogan exactly, with correct spelling and legible lettering.
* Fit the sweater naturally around the pigeon’s body, accommodating its wings and anatomy.
* Keep the pigeon’s head and distinctive natural features visible.
* Choose colors and patterns that complement the accessories.

The sweater should look like a real, slightly ridiculous miniature Christmas garment.

5. Christmas Accessories

Choose one or two complementary accessories that enhance the festive absurdity.

Possible accessories include:

* Reindeer antler headband: Plush brown antlers attached to a fabric headband, optionally decorated with holly, bells, or miniature ears.
* Santa hat: Red fabric hat with white fur trim and a fluffy pom-pom.
* Elf hat: Pointed green-and-red hat with a small golden bell.
* Christmas scarf: Knitted scarf with festive stripes or snowflakes.
* Christmas bow tie: Oversized plaid bow tie or red velvet ribbon.
* Jingle-bell collar: Small decorative golden bells.
* Holiday earmuffs: Miniature red or green earmuffs.
* Victorian caroler accessories: Tiny top hat, festive scarf, or velvet cape.
* Rudolph accessories: Plush reindeer antlers and a small red nose.

Accessory selection

* Choose a visually cohesive combination rather than adding as many decorations as possible.
* Vary the accessories across different generations.
* Avoid consistently defaulting to the same hat or headband.
* Accessories should look like real miniature costume pieces, fitted convincingly to the bird.
* Ensure accessories respect the pigeon’s anatomy and do not obscure its eyes, beak, or distinctive features.
* Use believable materials, textures, folds, and shadows.

The accessories should enhance the absurdity without overwhelming the pigeon.

6. Photographic Realism and Creative Direction

The transformed image must be convincingly photorealistic.

Photographic realism

* Match the original photograph’s lighting, exposure, shadows, and color temperature.
* Preserve realistic feather textures and natural iridescence.
* Render fabric with detailed stitching, natural folds, and believable contact with the bird.
* Ensure clothing follows the pigeon’s anatomy.
* Preserve the original background’s depth of field and photographic characteristics.
* Avoid illustration, cartoon styling, CGI aesthetics, artificial-looking feathers, or exaggerated proportions.

The clothing should appear to have been physically present when the original photograph was taken.

Creative direction

The intended humor is subtle, absurd, and ironic.

Imagine encountering an otherwise ordinary pigeon in its natural environment, except that it happens to be wearing an elaborate Christmas outfit.

Do not manufacture comedy through exaggerated expressions, theatrical poses, or additional visual gags.

Exercise creativity primarily through:

* The originality and humor of the sweater slogan.
* The sweater’s knitted patterns and colors.
* The selection and combination of accessories.
* Small details in the clothing that reward closer inspection.

Prioritize image preservation over creative variation.

The photograph should remain ordinary. The clothing should be extraordinary.

7. No Pigeon Found — Lo-Fi Internet Meme

If the original photograph contains no identifiable pigeon, do not attempt to create one.

Instead, return the original photograph with a large, humorous text overlay styled like an early-internet meme.

Image preservation

* Preserve the original photograph exactly as provided.
* Do not add, remove, replace, or modify any objects, animals, people, or environmental features.
* Do not change the background, lighting, colors, composition, or camera perspective.
* Do not introduce a pigeon, pigeon silhouette, cartoon bird, or other bird imagery.
* The only permitted change is the addition of the text overlay and minimal styling needed to make it readable.

Text content

Invent a short, original Christmas-themed bird pun about the absence of a pigeon.

* Make the message immediately understandable, slightly absurd, and funny.
* Draw inspiration from Christmas traditions, holiday songs, bird behavior, and the idea of searching unsuccessfully for a pigeon.
* Favor unexpected wordplay over predictable holiday phrases.
* Keep the message short and punchy, ideally 3–8 words.
* Avoid relying on familiar or overused bird puns.
* Do not reuse the same message across generations if previous outputs are available.
* Use uppercase lettering for maximum visual impact.

Generate several possible messages internally and select one for its originality, humor, and readability.

Do not display the alternatives or explain the selection.

Typography and visual treatment

The overlay should evoke an amateur internet meme from the late 1990s or early 2000s.

Think early personal websites, primitive image editors, pixel fonts, and deliberately crude digital graphics.

* Use very large, bold, blocky computer-style lettering.
* Favor pixelated bitmap fonts, chunky monospace fonts, or primitive system fonts.
* Use all caps for maximum impact.
* Make the text immediately readable on a mobile phone.
* Prefer one or two short lines.
* Use high-contrast colors such as bright white, yellow, cyan, lime green, or red.
* Apply a thick black outline, hard-edged drop shadow, or simple contrasting rectangular background when needed.
* Favor hard edges and flat colors over polished gradients or sophisticated effects.
* Allow slightly awkward spacing or deliberately unsophisticated placement.
* Position the text prominently, preferably across the upper or central portion of the image.
* Maintain the original image dimensions and aspect ratio.

The text should feel as though someone opened the photograph in a primitive image editor and added an oversized caption using the most ridiculous font available.

Avoid modern graphic design aesthetics.

Do not use elegant typography, cinematic titles, refined layouts, contemporary social-media templates, or professionally designed holiday-card styling.

The intended effect is crude, nostalgic, playful, and intentionally low-budget.

The humor should come from the absurdly dramatic caption applied to an otherwise completely ordinary photograph.

Additional constraints

* Do not add clip art, emojis, stickers, decorative borders, or illustrations.
* Do not add fake computer windows, browser chrome, or interface elements.
* Do not alter the underlying photograph to make it look retro.
* Apply the lo-fi treatment only to the text overlay, not to the photograph itself.

8. Final Output

Generate one high-quality image based on the uploaded photograph.

When a pigeon is present

* Preserve the pigeon’s recognizable identity and natural appearance.
* Preserve the original environment and photographic composition.
* Add a realistic knitted Christmas sweater.
* Include one clearly legible, original bird-related Christmas pun.
* Add one or two complementary Christmas accessories.
* Maintain realistic anatomy, lighting, and photographic detail.
* Do not add external captions, watermarks, or borders.

When no pigeon is present

* Preserve the original photograph without altering its visual content.
* Add one large, legible, original Christmas-themed bird pun about the missing pigeon.
* Render the text in a deliberately crude, lo-fi early-internet meme style.
* Do not generate or insert any pigeons or other animals.
* Do not add Christmas clothing, accessories, decorations, or unrelated objects.
* Maintain the original image dimensions and aspect ratio.

9. Final Priorities

Apply these priorities in order:

1. Pigeon detection: Accurately determine whether a pigeon is present.
2. Image preservation: Keep the original photograph as unchanged as possible.
3. Correct transformation: Dress an existing pigeon or add a missing-pigeon text overlay, never both.
4. Original humor: Invent fresh, funny bird-related Christmas puns without relying on a fixed list of examples.
5. Visual consistency: Maintain photographic realism for pigeon transformations and lo-fi typography for no-pigeon outcomes.

Success criteria

Pigeon present: The result should look like the original photograph, with the smallest possible set of changes needed to make the pigeon look absurdly festive. The humor emerges naturally from the contrast between an ordinary pigeon and its ridiculous Christmas clothing.

No pigeon present: The result should look like the original photograph with a large, hilariously crude early-internet Christmas meme caption acknowledging the pigeon’s absence.

In both cases, preserve the original image and let the absurdity speak for itself.
